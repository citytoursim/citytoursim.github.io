City Tour Simulator: Explore real cities

Fly between landmarks on narrated tours, walk the streets, and meet friends in the same city. Photorealistic 3D, with a new city every month.

Watch on YouTube Coming to Steam

A guide who flies you there

Pick a city and the camera glides from landmark to landmark while the narrator tells each story: when the Golden Gate opened, why it is orange, what happened on Alcatraz.

Short and long tours
A tour of 15 highlights when you have an evening, and one of 30 when you want the whole city.
Pause any time
Step out, walk around the neighbourhood, then pick the tour back up where you left it.
Always oriented
A minimap shows where you are and where the tour goes next. Subtitles are built in.

Leave the tour whenever you like

The city is not a backdrop. Land anywhere, go on foot, and see it the way you would if you were there.

Walk the streets

Drop to ground level and wander. Shop fronts, trees and kerbs are all there.

Climb onto rooftops

Ladders and ledges take you up for the view the street never gives you.

Take off with a jetpack

Skip the stairs. Lift above the rooftops and cross a neighbourhood in seconds.

Fast travel

Jump straight to the sights, then wander from there.

Explore with friends

Host a city and invite friends. Walk, fly and tour side by side.

Golden hour, any hour

Follow the clock from morning light to night, when street lamps and shop windows light up.
