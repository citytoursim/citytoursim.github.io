Narrated city tours

Every city has a short tour of 15 highlights and a long tour of 30. The camera glides to each landmark while the narrator tells its story. Pause anytime, step out and explore on foot, then pick the tour back up.

Real cities, explored your way

City Tour Simulator streams the real world in photorealistic 3D. Pick a city and go.

Walk, climb, fly

Walk the streets, climb onto rooftops, take to the sky with a jetpack, or let a guide fly you from landmark to landmark.

Fast travel and free roam

Jump straight to the sights, then wander. A minimap shows where you are and where the tour goes next.

Golden hour, any hour

Follow the clock from morning light to night, when street lamps and shop windows light up.

Explore together

Host a city and invite friends: walk, fly and tour side by side.

Made to be comfortable

Subtitles, keyboard, mouse and controller support. No violence, just exploring.
