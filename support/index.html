Pick the next city

We add a new city every month. Support the one you most want to explore: every contribution counts as a vote, and the city with the most support is the one we build next.

Opening soonPayments are not open yet. This page shows how the vote will work.

  1. Choose a city and give what you like, by card (including Apple Pay and Google Pay) or Bitcoin. Checkout opens on the payment provider's own page.
  2. Totals update on this page about every 30 minutes. Only the total and the number of supporters per city are shown, never names.
  3. At the end of each month the city with the most support goes into production as the next new city.

Support is a contribution to the project, not a purchase. You receive nothing in return, and there is no guarantee that a city will be added or when.
