How it works

  1. Choose a city and support the project with any amount, by card (including Apple Pay and Google Pay) or Bitcoin. Checkout opens on the payment provider's own page.
  2. One supporter, one vote per city per month, however much they give, so a single large payment cannot decide the vote. The amount shows separately as funding progress.
  3. When the month's vote closes, the city with the most votes is next, once we have checked its 3D coverage and quality. A city takes 6 to 8 weeks to make, so each vote decides the release about two months later.

Terms

  • Voluntary support, not a purchase. You are supporting the project. You are not buying a city, map content or anything else.
  • A vote, not a guarantee. We may skip or delay a city for coverage, quality or legal reasons.
  • Refunds as the law requires. Write to hello@citytoursim.com.
  • Public totals, private names. We publish the votes and amount per city, never supporters' names.
  • Payments are processed by Stripe for cards and by our Bitcoin checkout provider. We never see your card details.
